# Demo subjects

Blocks served with the site for the **Studio (the Atelier) only**. They are fetched when
opened there, never on a plain visit, and the public Viewer never shows them: leaving the
Studio goes back to the work.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `dancer.v4d` | A dancer **generated by the author with Kling AI 4.0: not a capture of a real person.** The clip was cropped to keep its watermark out of the tracked pixels, lifted into 3D tracks by ReLift on the author's PC (6 November 2026), and imported with `v4d_lab.relift import` at 1.5M points, 73 frames, 3 s. It carries tracks, what the camera saw (`observed`) and the raw lift (`rawPosition`), so TRACE, ANATOMY and WITNESS all work on it. See `docs/research/relift-report.md` §6.4. |

The block's own `meta.origin` says the same, and the Studio shows it (Source panel, and the
word *generated* beside the subject).
