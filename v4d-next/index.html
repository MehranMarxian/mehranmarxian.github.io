V4D

A photograph keeps one instant. V4D keeps a stretch of time: a body moving through a space, stored as points that each carry a place and a moment. Nothing about that time is decided when it is kept. It is decided afterwards, each time it is looked at.

The Timegraph lens is how it is looked at. It chooses how much time is held at once, how that time is laid into space, and what time does to what it holds. A photograph is writing with light; a timegraph is writing with time.

Ways of seeing

Now
One instant, as a photograph keeps it.
Echo
The present thickens into what has just passed, which is still here: what Husserl called retention, the comet's tail of the present; Bragaglia photographed it as a path rather than poses.
Firebrand
Rumi, Masnavi I:1144–1147: the world is renewed at every breath, and its continuity is an appearance made by speed, like a whirled firebrand seen as one line of fire. Watch the spark become a line as the time held grows.
Dissolve
The present holds; before and after come apart into dust.
Exposure
The shutter never closes, as in Hiroshi Sugimoto's theatres and Michael Wesely's years-long exposures.

Touching it

Drag to turn the body in space. Scroll or pinch to come closer. Press and hold to stop time; let go and it runs again. The words along the bottom change the way of seeing; so do the keys 1 to 5. Space stops and starts time, F fills the screen, H hides the words.

Every number behind the lens is in the studio (key S).

Your own time

Sit, the last word along the bottom (key 6), opens your own camera, only when you ask: first a mirror with memory, then, when you are ready, a sitting of twenty seconds, as for a daguerreotype. Hold still; the sitting develops into one image of all its time, which you may save. The camera closes when the sitting ends. Everything happens on your device: nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing is kept unless you save it.

The figure

The figure walking here is a stand-in: a stock character from Adobe Mixamo, walking a stock clip. The first sitters, people kept in time from a camera, come next.

Lineage
