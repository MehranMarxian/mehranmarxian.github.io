Mehran Ahmadi is a visual artist and photographer whose practice spans traditional image-making and computational media. Working across photography, photogrammetry, computer vision, and generative systems, he builds visual narratives that interrogate how we perceive, reconstruct, and reimagine the human figure in an era of machine sight.
